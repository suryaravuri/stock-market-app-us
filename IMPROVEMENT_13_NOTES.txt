Improvement #13 - Signal Expiration / Staleness Protection

Adds an EXPIRED lifecycle state and blocks stale setups from becoming actionable.

Rules:
- 15Min setup freshness limit: 45 minutes (timeframe-aware defaults are defined in signal_staleness.py).
- WATCHING/BREAKOUT_PENDING/FOLLOW_THROUGH_PENDING expire when their signal candle belongs to a prior market session.
- Same-day unconfirmed signals expire after the regular session closes (America/New_York, 16:00).
- BREAKOUT_PENDING/FOLLOW_THROUGH_PENDING expire after 4 newer regular-session candles without progression.
- ENTRY_CONFIRMED is intentionally excluded; confirmed positions/signals belong to a later position-management lifecycle.

New response diagnostics:
expired, staleness_status, expiration_reason, signal_age_minutes,
max_signal_age_minutes, pending_candles, max_pending_candles.

When expired, /setup returns action=SIGNAL_EXPIRED and actionable=false.
No database migration is required because signal_state.state is already a string column and history can store EXPIRED.
