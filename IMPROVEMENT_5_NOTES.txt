Improvement #5 - Breakout Confirmation

Adds a read-only breakout confirmation endpoint:
GET /api/v1/analysis/{symbol}/breakout-confirmation?timeframe=15Min

Confirmation requires:
1. Latest completed candle closes above the highest high of the prior 20 candles.
2. A 0.05 ATR price buffer above that breakout level.
3. Latest volume >= 1.20x prior 20-candle average volume.
4. Latest candle closes above its open.

Statuses:
- WAITING_FOR_PRICE
- PRICE_BREAKOUT_LOW_VOLUME
- PRICE_BREAKOUT_WEAK_CANDLE
- BREAKOUT_CONFIRMED
- INSUFFICIENT_DATA

This layer does not submit orders and does not enable trading.
Existing Improvement #1-#4 behavior is preserved.
