Improvement #10.1 - Breakout diagnostic consistency

- failed_quality_checks is now populated even before price confirmation.
- Adds CANDLE_NOT_BULLISH, BODY_TOO_SMALL, CLOSE_NOT_NEAR_HIGH,
  UPPER_WICK_TOO_LARGE, and LOW_BREAKOUT_VOLUME when applicable.
- ENTRY_CONFIRMED responses now report the original breakout candle's
  breakout level/confirmation metrics rather than the follow-through candle's
  independently calculated breakout baseline.
