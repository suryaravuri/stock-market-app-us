Improvement #8 - Session/Liquidity-Aware Breakout Candle Selection

Breakout confirmation now selects the latest eligible completed US regular-session
15-minute candle instead of blindly using the newest stored bar.

Eligibility rules:
- US regular session only: candle start >= 09:30 and < 16:00 America/New_York
- structurally valid OHLC/VWAP/positive volume
- non-zero candle range
- trade_count >= 10 when trade_count is available
- candidate volume >= 10% of the preceding 20 eligible-candle average

If the newest bar is a thin 16:00 boundary/after-hours candle, selection automatically
walks backward to the most recent eligible candle. Breakout confirmation still requires
1.20x average volume to become BREAKOUT_CONFIRMED; the 10% threshold is only a data/
liquidity eligibility floor.

Trading and paper-order switches remain unchanged/disabled.
