#15H-5 Controlled Diagnostics Simulation

Adds POST /api/v1/analysis/trade-journal/diagnostics/simulate.

Purpose:
- Validate #15H weakness detection with controlled hypothetical completed trades.
- Detect weak/non-positive expectancy, poor profit factor, low win rate,
  stop-exit concentration, and weak symbol/timeframe buckets without waiting
  for real production analytical trade history.

Safety/isolation:
- simulation_mode=STATELESS_DIAGNOSTICS_VALIDATION
- execution_mode=ANALYSIS_ONLY
- persistence_mode=NONE
- database_mutated=false
- production_position_tables_mutated=false
- broker_order_submitted=false
- No Alembic migration required.

Input trade fields:
- realized_r (required)
- symbol (default SIMULATION)
- timeframe (default 15Min)
- state (optional; inferred when absent)
- exit_reason (optional; inferred when absent)
- holding_minutes (default 60)
