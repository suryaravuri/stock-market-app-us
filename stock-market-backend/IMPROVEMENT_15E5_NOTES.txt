#15E-5 Source-Aware Analytics

Purpose
-------
Prevent isolated synthetic position tests from mixing their synthetic OHLC prices with real market_candle rows when computing MFE, MAE, giveback, and exit efficiency.

Changes
-------
1. New Alembic revision: e15e5a90c2b1 (down_revision c41a8e2d90f1).
2. position_test_state.analytics_candle_source identifies the authoritative candle source.
3. New position_test_candle_observation table persists every evaluated synthetic OHLC candle from #15C-2 controlled persistent tests.
4. New synthetic runs use SYNTHETIC_PERSISTED_CANDLES.
5. #15D real-candle runs use MARKET_CANDLE.
6. Existing #15D real-candle test runs are backfilled to MARKET_CANDLE using their immutable opening-history event.
7. Existing pre-#15E-5 synthetic runs remain SYNTHETIC_UNRECORDED because their full OHLC sequence cannot be reconstructed safely.
8. Analytics response adds analytics_candle_source, source_data_status, and source_data_note.
9. For SYNTHETIC_UNRECORDED historical runs, realized P&L remains available from entry/exit, but MFE/MAE/giveback/exit-efficiency are intentionally null rather than using real market data.
10. No broker behavior changed. execution_mode remains ANALYSIS_ONLY.
