#15H Strategy Diagnostics & Weakness Analysis

Adds GET /api/v1/analysis/trade-journal/diagnostics.

Safety / evidence rules:
- ANALYSIS_ONLY and read-only; no DB mutation or broker action.
- Only PRODUCTION_ANALYTICAL completed trades drive diagnostics.
- ISOLATED_TEST rows are counted for transparency but cannot create strengths,
  hide weaknesses, or affect recommendations.
- No database migration required.

Diagnostics include:
- sample-size weakness
- average-R / expectancy weakness
- profit-factor weakness
- win-rate/payoff interaction
- stop-exit concentration
- weak symbol buckets
- weak timeframe buckets
- prioritized evidence-backed recommendations
- strengths when thresholds are genuinely supported

With no production analytical trades, the correct result is NOT_EVALUABLE with
NO_REAL_TRADE_EVIDENCE, even if isolated test trades exist.
