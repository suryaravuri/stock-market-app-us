# Improvement #15G - Strategy Performance & Quality Score

Adds source-aware strategy quality evaluation on top of #15F.

Endpoint:
GET /api/v1/analysis/trade-journal/quality

Key safety/design rule:
- Only completed PRODUCTION_ANALYTICAL positions contribute to the strategy quality score.
- ISOLATED_TEST / synthetic engineering validation trades are counted and explicitly excluded.
- A quality grade is withheld until at least 15 real analytical trades exist.
- Confidence remains LOW until 30 real analytical trades and rises with sample size.
- Read-only; ANALYSIS_ONLY; no broker calls; no database mutation.

Score components (100 max):
- sample size: 40
- average R / expectancy: 30
- R-based profit factor: 20
- win rate: 10

The score is intentionally conservative: small winning samples cannot receive a trusted grade.
No Alembic migration required.
