Signal lifecycle terminal-cycle timestamp fix
Date: 2026-08-31

Problem
-------
A terminal EXPIRED/INVALIDATED signal cycle could reject re-arming but still
have signal_candle_time overwritten by the latest analytical observation.
This produced contradictory output such as an EXPIRED prior-session reason
paired with a current-session signal_candle_time.

Fix
---
- Added should_refresh_signal_candle_time() lifecycle policy.
- Active cycles continue to refresh signal_candle_time.
- A real lifecycle transition (including transition into INVALIDATED/EXPIRED)
  records the candle that caused the transition.
- An already-terminal cycle that does not transition/re-arm preserves its
  original cycle signal_candle_time.
- No trading, entry-gate, re-arm qualification, or broker behavior changed.

-------------------

- signal_state should remain EXPIRED (or existing terminal state)
- signal_candle_time should remain the original terminal-cycle candle
- analysis_as_of may advance to the latest completed regular-session candle
- rearm_status should indicate SETUP_NOT_QUALIFIED
