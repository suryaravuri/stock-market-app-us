Improvement #15B - Position Lifecycle Simulation & Validation

Goal
----
Validate the post-entry lifecycle with controlled completed candles without changing
real market data, signal state, position state, or broker orders.

New endpoint
------------
POST /api/v1/analysis/{symbol}/position-lifecycle/simulate

Safety boundary
---------------
- simulation_mode = STATELESS_VALIDATION
- execution_mode = ANALYSIS_ONLY
- broker_order_submitted = false
- database_mutated = false
- No DB Session dependency in the simulation endpoint.
- No Alpaca/broker client is called.

Validation
----------
The request validates long entry/stop/target ordering, positive/valid OHLC values,
and strictly increasing candle timestamps.

The simulation uses the same evaluate_long_position_candle() strategy as the
persistent Improvement #15 lifecycle, so controlled tests exercise the real state
transition rules rather than a duplicate implementation.

-------------

The trail becomes 102.5 after the third candle and is touched by the fourth.
