Improvement #15E - Position P&L and Exit Analytics

Adds read-only computed analytics for persistent analytical positions and isolated test positions.

Endpoints:
GET /api/v1/analysis/{symbol}/position-lifecycle/analytics?timeframe=15Min
GET /api/v1/analysis/{symbol}/position-lifecycle/persistent-test/{test_run_id}/analytics

Metrics (PER_SHARE basis):
- unrealized P&L and R for open positions
- realized P&L and R for terminal positions
- MFE / MAE per share and in R
- best / worst observed post-entry price
- giveback from MFE and exit efficiency % for closed positions
- lifecycle holding minutes
- candles analyzed and out-of-session exclusions

Safety:
- ANALYSIS_ONLY
- read-only; no database mutation
- no broker calls
- production_position_tables_mutated=false
- no Alembic migration required

Note: total-dollar P&L is intentionally not calculated because position quantity is not stored in the lifecycle record yet.
