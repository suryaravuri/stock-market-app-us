Improvement #10 - Follow-Through / Retest Confirmation

- A qualifying breakout candle is now BREAKOUT_DETECTED_PENDING_FOLLOW_THROUGH and is NOT actionable.
- The next qualified regular-session candle must either:
  * hold completely above the old breakout level, or
  * retest the breakout zone and close back above the buffered confirmation price.
- Only then status becomes ENTRY_CONFIRMED and confirmed=true.
- Failed follow-through returns FOLLOW_THROUGH_FAILED.
- Added structured failed_quality_checks and follow-through diagnostics.
- Decision engine maps final confirmed entry to action=ENTRY_CONFIRMED.
- No trading switches were enabled.
