Improvement #11 - Confirmed breakout risk recalculation

When follow-through/retest produces ENTRY_CONFIRMED:
- Entry = completed follow-through/retest candle close.
- Stop anchor = lower of breakout level and follow-through candle low.
- Stop = anchor - 0.10 ATR.
- Targets prefer structural resistance above the confirmed entry.
- If no structural resistance exists above entry, targets fall back to 2R/3R
  planning levels (explicitly labeled R_MULTIPLE_FALLBACK; not a forecast).
- Setup risk_reward now exposes recalculated_after_breakout, calculation_basis,
  and target_source.
- Position sizing automatically consumes the recalculated entry/stop because it
  already uses SetupService risk_reward output.
