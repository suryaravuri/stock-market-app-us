Improvement #4 - Action Decision Layer

Added operational setup actions:
- BUY_SETUP
- WAIT_FOR_BREAKOUT
- WAIT_FOR_PULLBACK
- NEUTRAL
- AVOID
- UNAVAILABLE (scanner error state)

Decision order:
1. AVOID when multi-timeframe classification/score is bearish.
2. NEUTRAL when overall score is below 30.
3. WAIT_FOR_BREAKOUT when positive trend is within 0.25 ATR of overhead resistance.
4. WAIT_FOR_PULLBACK when risk/reward is POOR.
5. WAIT_FOR_PULLBACK when price is more than 1 ATR above nearest support.
6. BUY_SETUP when trend score is >=30, R:R is GOOD/ACCEPTABLE, and no blocker above applies.

Setup and scanner responses now include:
- action
- decision_reasons
- actionable

Position-size setup summary also includes action and decision_reasons.

This layer makes decisions only; it does not place orders.
